How the solver decides

It runs expectimax. For each of your four moves it imagines every cell where the next tile could appear, as a 2 (90%) or a 4 (10%), then your best reply, and so on, a few moves deep. Lines that are very unlikely are cut short. Each position at the end is scored for empty cells, tiles lined up in order, and neighbours ready to merge. The bars show each move's expected score relative to the best one.

The search deepens until the thinking time runs out, so longer thinking reads further ahead. The same engine as a native C program reached 16384 on 4×4 in testing.
